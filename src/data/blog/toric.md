# About this seminar

Toric varieties are special geometric objects which are defined using combinatorial information. This combinatorial data can be used to compute many geometric properties of toric varieties. Even though toric varieties are quite special, many general phenomena can be observed on them. In this semester, we will discuss the basic geometry of toric varieties and then see their applications in algebraic geometry and other areas of mathematics. For example, applications of toric varieties to results in combinatorics are discussed in Chapter 5 of **[F]** and in Chapter 4 of **[O]**.

# Outline of the seminar

**Fundamental topics:**
- Crash course on algebraic varieties
- Polyhedral cones. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 1.2
- Affine toric varieties. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 1.3
- Fans and general toric varieties. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 1.4
- Toric varieties from polytopes. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 1.5
- Local properties. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 2.1. Ignore the stuff about toric varieties being Cohen-Macaulay -- this is a technical condition in commutative algebra.
- Toric surfaces. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 2.2.
- 1-parameter subgroups and limit points, compactness. _Reference:_ **[F]**, sections 2.3, 2.4.
- Smooth toric surfaces. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 2.5
- Toric resolution of singularities. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 2.6; **[CLS]**, sections 10.1, 11.1.
- Orbits of the torus action. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 3.1
- Topological properties. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 3.2
- Divisors and line bundles. _Reference:_ **[F]**, sections 3.3, 3.4
- Tangent bundle, canonical divisor. _Reference:_ **[F]**, section 4.3; **[CLS]**, chapter 8.

**More advanced topics you may choose to talk about if you are interested, listed roughly in order of increasing difficulty. Note that some of these will take several talks. You can also choose a topic not on this list if you want.**
- A compact but not projective toric variety. _Reference:_  **[CLS]**, example 6.3.26.
- Applications to combinatorics. _Reference:_ **[F]**, sections 5.3, 5.4, 5.6, **[CLS]**, section 10.5
- Toric varieties as quotients and the secondary fan. _Reference:_ **[CLS]**, sections 5.0, 5.1, chapters 14, 15.
- Reflexive polytopes and Fano toric varieties. _Reference:_ **[CLS]**, section 5.1, chapters 14, 15.
- Application of the above to mathematical physics. _Reference:_ Victor Batyrev, [_Dual polyhedra and mirror symmetry for Calabi-Yau hypersurfaces in toric varieties._](https://arxiv.org/abs/alg-geom/9310003)
