Five candidates

Each is a complete home page for plei99.github.io, built from the site's real content, and each tries a different way for a visitor to find the sections. Click a thumbnail to open it full size. They are mockups: content is trimmed, and the seminar links go nowhere.

Opened with About and Now; press 2 for Seminars.

1. Tiles a tiling window manager

The page is a workspace. Each section is a window; open several and they split the screen in a dwindle layout, as hyprland would. The empty workspace is a wallpaper that shows the keymap, so nothing is hidden.

  • Press 1–7 to open sections, h/l to move focus, f to zoom, q to close.
  • Follows your system light or dark setting (Everforest).
  • The URL hash records the open windows, so a layout can be linked.
Open Tiles

The first chapter; the route draws as you scroll.

2. Route a scroll-driven journey

One long page, read top to bottom, structured as the biography: Amherst, New York, Boston, Shanghai, elsewhere. A schematic route stays pinned beside the text and draws itself as the chapters pass, with the marker at the stop you are reading.

  • Scroll. Or use the stop list at the top right to jump.
  • Seminars hang off the city where they ran; the Now list lives at Shanghai.
  • EB Garamond at display sizes; survey-map blue grey and cobalt.
Open Route

The About sheet at the front of the folder.

3. Dossier a folder with index tabs

A manila folder on a desk. Each section is a typed sheet, and the tab you press brings it to the front; the other sheets stay visible behind it, so the size of the file is never a mystery. A paperclip holds the photo.

  • Click the tabs, or use ← → (a proper tablist) and 1–7.
  • Typewriter face (Courier Prime, loaded from Google Fonts for the mockup).
  • The one candidate with a physical metaphor; test whether it reads as charming or as a gimmick.
Open Dossier

The index on the left; the page it cites on the right.

4. Index the back of a book

The home page is a book's index: every name, place and topic on the site, alphabetically, with the page it appears on. Choose an entry and the facing page turns to that passage with the term marked. Discovery is alphabetical rather than by category, and a visitor sees at a glance whether you mention them.

  • Click entries; try Sichuan cuisine, moduli space, whether I am a good, or a person's name.
  • Previous and next page at the bottom of the right page.
  • On a phone the page becomes a sheet that rises from the bottom.
Open Index

At About, the interchange between the two lines.

5. Transit a metro map

The site drawn as a metro map. The Research line runs About, Seminars, Notes, arXiv; the Life line runs About, Travel, Now, Uses; a short shuttle reaches Contact. Choosing a station opens a signage-style card, and the arrows ride the line to the next station.

  • Click stations, or press ← → to travel along the current line.
  • Hover a line to isolate it. Stations off the site are marked as exits.
  • Like your current figure, the diagram is the site map; unlike it, the idiom is civic rather than mathematical.
Open Transit

All five keep every section reachable from the first screen without a loading step, sound, or motion that must finish first. Fonts come from public/fonts; the photo from public/images. Serve the repository root and open /experiments/.
