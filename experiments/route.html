Western Massachusetts

I started in Amherst.

At UMass Amherst I was advised by Paul Hacking, and also majored in computer science. Both habits stuck: I study geometric and computational aspects of enumerative geometry, and I still write a lot of code.

If you don't know me from Amherst, we may have met at a conference or a seminar.
