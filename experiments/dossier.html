About sheet 1 of 7

I am a postdoc at the Shanghai Center for Mathematical Sciences at Fudan University. Previously, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor (postdoc) in mathematics at Boston College, mentored by Qile Chen. I completed my PhD at Columbia University, advised by Melissa Liu.

My research is in algebraic geometry, particularly higher-genus Gromov–Witten theory. I study both geometric and computational aspects of enumerative geometry. See these notes or this SCGP program for an introduction.

You may also know me from UMass Amherst, where I was advised by Paul Hacking and also majored in computer science. Or we may have met at a conference or a seminar.
